This Agent Compensation Agreement outlines the compensation structure for agents working with Talent Nation and provides a clear and transparent understanding of how monthly agent compensation and performance adjustments are calculated.

Talent Nation believes that creator development is a shared responsibility. Our goal is for agency leadership and agents to work together to help creators remain active, grow their LIVE presence, and reach meaningful performance milestones.

1Base Agent Compensation

4%  of eligible Diamonds earned by your team each month

Agents are eligible to receive compensation equal to 4% of the total eligible Diamonds earned during the applicable month by creators assigned to their team.

For purposes of this agreement, "eligible Diamonds" means Diamonds earned by creators for whom Talent Nation qualifies for and receives the applicable agency compensation from TikTok.

2Creator Eligibility

For a creator's Diamonds to be included in an agent's monthly compensation calculation, the creator must be considered in good standing with Talent Nation for the applicable payment period.

Good standing includes meeting TikTok's applicable activeness requirements, including:

  • At least 20 LIVE hours
  • Across at least 8 qualifying LIVE days

If a creator does not meet the applicable activeness requirements and Talent Nation does not receive agency compensation from TikTok for that creator, that creator's Diamonds will not be included when calculating the agent's compensation.

3Released Creators

A creator must remain an active member of Talent Nation and must not have been released from the agency for their Diamonds to qualify for agent compensation.

Once a creator is released from Talent Nation, the agency is no longer compensated for that creator's subsequent Diamonds. Therefore, Diamonds earned after the creator's effective release date will not be included in the agent's compensation calculation.

Any compensation associated with a creator is subject to Talent Nation actually receiving the corresponding agency compensation from TikTok.

4Agent Good Standing

Agents must remain in good standing with Talent Nation to be eligible for monthly compensation and performance bonuses.

Good standing includes fulfilling the responsibilities and expectations of the agent role, following Talent Nation's policies and procedures, maintaining appropriate communication with creators and agency leadership, and complying with applicable TikTok Creator Network requirements and policies.

5Graduation & Maturation

Creator development and graduation are important components of both Talent Nation's success and the agent compensation structure.

Creator Graduation200,000Diamonds in a single calendar month within their first 90 days of joining Talent Nation
Creator Maturation200,000Diamonds again in the immediately following calendar month after Graduating

A qualifying Maturation also counts toward the agent's Graduation performance metric. Creators do not need to mature; some may only graduate and never mature. Premium invites can't graduate, they can only mature.

6Graduation Rate Performance Adjustment

TikTok's agency compensation is affected by creator Graduation performance. Because Talent Nation may receive a reduced agency payout when required Graduation performance is not achieved, an agent's compensation rate may also be adjusted based on the Graduation performance of their assigned team.

Below 10% Graduation Rate — 1% Reduction

If an agent's team achieves a Graduation rate of less than 10% during the applicable measurement period, the agent's standard compensation rate will be reduced by 1 percentage point for that applicable payment period. This adjustment reflects the corresponding impact that insufficient Graduation performance has on compensation received by Talent Nation.

The purpose of this adjustment is not to place the responsibility for creator performance solely on the agent. Talent Nation leadership and agents will work together to identify creators who are approaching Graduation, develop strategies to support them, provide coaching and resources, and help as many eligible creators as possible reach Graduation.

10% Graduation Rate — Standard Compensation

If an agent's team achieves a 10% Graduation rate, the agent remains eligible for their standard 4% base compensation, subject to all other requirements contained in this agreement.

Greater Than 10% Graduation Rate — Additional 0.5%

If an agent's team achieves a Graduation rate of more than 10%, the agent will qualify for an additional 0.5 percentage point Graduation Performance Bonus, before any additional Team Diamond Performance Bonus is applied. Qualifying Graduations and Maturations will count toward the Graduation performance calculation.

9Monthly Payment Schedule

Agent compensation will generally be paid on the 15th of the month following the month in which the eligible Diamonds were earned, after Talent Nation receives and verifies the applicable payment and reporting from TikTok.

10Voluntary Departure & Final Compensation

Agents may choose to end their agent relationship with Talent Nation at any time.

If an agent chooses to leave Talent Nation, they will remain eligible for compensation on qualifying Diamonds earned by their eligible assigned creators through the agent's final active date with Talent Nation, subject to all other eligibility requirements contained in this agreement.

The departing agent's final compensation will be calculated using eligible Diamonds earned through their final active date and will be paid according to Talent Nation's normal payment schedule after the applicable TikTok payment has been received and verified.

The agent will not receive compensation for Diamonds earned by those creators after the agent's final active date, even if those creators remain with Talent Nation.

Leaving the agent role does not result in the forfeiture of compensation already earned and otherwise payable under this agreement through the agent's final active date.

11Payment Calculation & TikTok Payments

Agent compensation is based on eligible performance data and payments received by Talent Nation. Because TikTok determines creator eligibility, agency qualification, Diamond totals, and final agency payouts, Talent Nation may reconcile agent compensation against TikTok's finalized reporting before issuing payment.

If TikTok later adjusts, reverses, withholds, or determines that certain creator activity or Diamonds are ineligible for agency compensation, Talent Nation may make corresponding adjustments to the agent compensation calculation.

12Additional Incentives

Talent Nation may offer additional bonuses, contests, incentives, or performance-based compensation opportunities.

Unless specifically stated otherwise in writing, these incentives are separate from the agent's standard compensation and may have their own eligibility requirements, measurement periods, and payment terms.
